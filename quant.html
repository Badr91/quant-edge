The Engine Architecture

Our system operates on a three-layer validation process.

Layer 1: Structural Detection

The system monitors the market for Market Structure Shifts (MSS) and Break of Structure (BOS). We only trade when the institutional trend has been mathematically confirmed.

Layer 2: Liquidity Validation

We look for Liquidity Sweeps (Stop Hunts) and Fair Value Gaps (FVG). The system identifies the "Trap" before entering the trade at an Order Block.

Layer 3: Quant Filtering

Every signal is filtered through a quantitative layer: VWAP, Z-Score (Mean Reversion), and Volume Strength. If the quant filter disagrees with the price action, the trade is discarded.

The Golden Rule: Risk Management

Intelligence is useless without survival. Our framework enforces strict capital preservation.

Fixed Risk Per Trade

We never risk more than 0.5% to 1% of total equity on a single setup. This ensures a "statistical edge" can play out over hundreds of trades.

Asymmetric RR

We target a minimum of 1:3 Reward-to-Risk. By keeping losses small and wins large, our win rate can be as low as 30% while remaining highly profitable.

Dynamic De-risking

Once a trade reaches 1:1 RR, we move the Stop Loss to break-even. We trade the market's reality, not our expectations.
